Progressive search

Search & Refine

Build your set step by step. Separate search terms with commas. For each step, choose where to search: Anywhere, Headword, Synonyms, Card Content, or Classification. Choose ALL when every term should match, or ANY when one or more may match.

Example give, phrasal verb · ALL → Refine → business, communication · ANY

Headword searches the Word field; Synonyms searches only Synonym(s). Card Content covers IPA, part of speech, definition, examples, notes, Category, and Source. Classification searches Entry Type, Usage, Topic, and Discovery. Anywhere searches all of them. The order of terms inside one Search step does not change priority.
